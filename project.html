THE PROJECT

About The Story

Every night, millions of people dream without knowing where those dreams go. But somewhere beyond ordinary sleep is a city built from those dreams.


The city changes every night. Buildings appear. Streets move. New residents arrive. Some people disappear. And recently, the citizens have begun remembering their dreamers.
