The City That Only Exists While You Sleep

A mysterious shared dream-city connects strangers across the world. As the dreamers begin to recognize the same streets and impossible locations, they realize the city may not be a dream at all.

Story Package

The core narrative foundation of the project.

LOGLINE

COMPLETE
Every night, strangers around the world enter the same impossible city in their dreams — and eventually discover that something inside the city knows they are awake.

SERIES PREMISE

The dream-city becomes a shared mystery. Each episode reveals another section of the city while introducing new dreamers who may have connections they don't understand in waking life.

CENTRAL QUESTION

What happens when people discover that the place they visit in their dreams exists independently of their minds?

TONE

DEFINED
Atmospheric · Mysterious · Psychological · Emotional · Surreal · Cinematic

SEASON ONE

DEVELOPING
Eight episodes following several dreamers as their separate experiences slowly reveal a connected mythology.

Character Package

The people who carry the dream into the story.

MAYA The first dreamer to realize the city exists beyond her own dreams.
ELI A stranger who recognizes Maya despite never meeting her in waking life.
THE ARCHIVIST A mysterious figure documenting every person who enters the city.
THE CHILD A recurring dreamer who seems to understand the city's rules before everyone else.
THE DRIVER A figure who transports dreamers between locations that should not exist.
UNKNOWN Character slot reserved for future development.

FORMAT CAN EVOLVE

A Dream World project does not necessarily have to remain in its original format. A concept may become a film, series, audio drama, book, game or another form if the creator and development team determine that a different format better serves the project.

Audience & Positioning

Who the project is for and how it could be presented.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE

Viewers interested in psychological mystery, science fiction, dream logic, supernatural stories and serialized mysteries.

POSITIONING

A serialized mystery built around the universal experience of dreaming, with an expandable mythology designed for long-term storytelling.

PLATFORM

Potential CrowRules TV original, subject to project development, funding, production and distribution decisions.

FUTURE POTENTIAL

Series · Audio · Books · Shorts · Interactive experiences · Merchandising and other opportunities, where appropriate and separately agreed.

Visual Development

The visual language that defines the dream.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

DEVELOPING
Dark cinematic environments, impossible architecture, empty streets, surreal lighting and subtle visual details that reveal the city is not behaving normally.

COLOR / LIGHT

CONCEPT
Nighttime environments with controlled contrast, dreamlike highlights and visual transitions between waking reality and the shared dream world.

KEY ART

IN PROGRESS
A single impossible city street disappearing into darkness, with several distant figures who may or may not be real.
