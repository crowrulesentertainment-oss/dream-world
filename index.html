DREAMSCAPES

One Dream. One World. One Story.

Everyone has a dream. A story they have imagined. A character they can't stop thinking about. A world that exists somewhere in their mind. Maybe it's only a sentence. Maybe it's pages of notes. Maybe it's something they've carried with them for years.

Dreamscapes is where that idea can begin to become real.

Bring us your dream, your concept, your characters, your strange idea, or the story you've always wanted to tell. Together, we can explore it, develop it, build its world, create its characters, shape its lore, write its story, and discover what it could become.

And you don't have to build it alone. Dreamscapes brings together writers, artists, world-builders, storytellers, filmmakers, designers, researchers, producers and other dreamers who want to create.

The original dreamer remains at the heart of the journey. If you want to be involved, you can collaborate with the people helping develop your idea every step of the way.

✦ VOLUNTEER CREATOR NETWORK

Volunteer Today. Grow With The Dream.

When you join the Dreamscapes Creator Network right now, you are joining as a volunteer creator.

You can contribute your ideas, skills, creativity, experience and time to appropriate Dreamscapes projects and collaborative development.

At this stage, participation is unpaid and voluntary. Joining Dreamscapes does not guarantee employment, compensation, a paid position, or that a particular project will eventually be produced.

01 START TOGETHER

Help build Dreamscapes while the network is still growing.

02 CREATE

Bring your creative skills into collaborative projects.

03 CONNECT

Meet other writers, artists, filmmakers and dreamers.

04 GROW

Continue developing your creative experience as the network evolves.

WHAT COULD HAPPEN LATER?

As CrowRules Entertainment grows, develops revenue and begins producing more projects, new opportunities may become possible.

Depending on the company's needs and available resources, future opportunities could include paid creative positions, freelance work, production roles, contracts, project-based work or other compensated opportunities.

Those opportunities are not guaranteed. They would be separate opportunities with their own terms, requirements and compensation.

IMPORTANT Volunteer participation is optional. Only contribute time, ideas or creative work that you are comfortable providing without current compensation.

HOW DREAMSCAPES WORKS

The idea is only the beginning.

Projects can grow through a series of creative stages, with room for discovery along the way.

01

Submit The Dream

Share the idea, story, world, character or concept you've been carrying around.

02

Discover The Possibilities

Explore what the idea could become, what kind of story it might tell and what creative direction makes sense.

03

Build The World

Develop characters, locations, lore, histories, rules, concepts and the details that make the world feel alive.

04

Bring In Collaborators

Writers, artists, designers, filmmakers and other creators can join when their skills fit the project's needs.

05

Write And Develop

Shape the concept into stories, scripts, documents, pitches, visual concepts and other development materials.

06

Explore Production

Some projects may eventually move toward production, depending on readiness, resources, opportunities and creative goals.

07

Share The Story

When a project reaches its destination, the goal is to bring the finished creation to an audience.
