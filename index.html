DREAMSCAPES

One Dream. One World. One Story.

Everyone has a dream. A story they have imagined. A character they have created. A world that exists somewhere inside their mind.

Dreamscapes is where those ideas begin to take shape. Submit your dream, develop the concept, write the lore, build the world, collaborate with other creators and, when the time is right, explore what that story could become through CrowRules Entertainment.

The Bigger Universe

Dreamscapes is part of CrowRules Entertainment.

Dreamscapes is designed to be the creative-development side of the CrowRules universe — a place where ideas can begin before they become finished productions.

CrowRules Entertainment is building the larger entertainment ecosystem. Dreamscapes is one of the places where the stories that could eventually live inside that ecosystem can begin.

The Journey

From an idea to a world.

Dreamscapes isn't about simply submitting an idea and walking away. The goal is to create a collaborative development journey around the people and ideas behind each dream.

01 / DREAM

Imagine It

Submit the idea, dream, character, story concept or world that has been living in your imagination.

02 / DEVELOP

Build It

Explore characters, locations, mythology, history, themes, rules and everything that makes the world feel alive.

03 / COLLABORATE

Create Together

Writers, artists, designers, filmmakers and other creators can work alongside the original dreamer.

04 / PRODUCE

Bring It to Life

Some projects may eventually move toward production or another CrowRules Entertainment creative format.

The Network

You don't have to build the dream alone.

Great worlds can take many different kinds of creators. Dreamscapes is intended to connect people who want to contribute their talents to the development process.

W

Writers

Help turn concepts into scripts, scenes, dialogue, characters, lore and complete stories.

A

Artists & Designers

Explore visual identities, characters, locations, concepts, posters and worlds.

F

Filmmakers

Help imagine how Dreamscapes projects could move from the page into film, television or digital production.

L

Lore Builders

Develop histories, mythology, timelines, cultures, technology and the deeper rules of a world.

C

Creative Collaborators

Bring ideas, feedback, brainstorming, research and different creative perspectives.

P

Future Production Teams

As CrowRules grows, some projects may eventually involve larger production teams and professional opportunities.

Creator First

The dreamer stays connected to the dream.

The original person who submits an idea can remain involved throughout development if they want to be. Dreamscapes is designed around collaboration rather than taking an idea and disappearing with it.

  • Participate in development discussions.
  • Help shape characters and world-building.
  • Work with other creators.
  • Follow the project's development journey.
