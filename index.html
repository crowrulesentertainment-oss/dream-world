THE DREAMSCAPES IDEA

Every great story starts somewhere.

It could start with a dream you had last night. A character you've carried in your head for years. A world you've never known how to build. A scene you can't stop imagining. Dreamscapes gives those ideas a place to begin.

THE MISSION

From imagination to creation.

Dreamscapes connects original dreamers with people who love building stories. The person who submits an idea doesn't have to disappear once the idea is submitted.

If they want to participate, they can remain involved throughout development. Helping shape the characters, lore, scripts, world and direction of their project.

✦
Your idea starts the journey. Dreams, concepts and original ideas can become the foundation for something much bigger.
◈
Collaboration builds the world. Writers, artists, designers and storytellers can help transform the original concept.
∞
Production can take it further. Projects may eventually move toward CrowRules Entertainment production opportunities.
THE DREAM LIBRARY

A growing universe of possibilities.

Dreamscapes is designed to become a living library of original concepts and developing worlds.

Some ideas may become short stories. Some may become screenplays. Some may become series, films, games, books, characters or completely new creative experiences.

The destination is not always known when the dream begins.

THE JOURNEY

Dream. Build. Develop. Create.

Dreamscapes is built around a collaborative creative development process.

01 · DREAM

Submit the idea

Share the dream, concept, character, world or story you want to explore.

02 · BUILD

Build the world

Develop characters, settings, lore, mythology, concepts and the foundations of the project.

03 · DEVELOP

Write together

Turn concepts into outlines, stories, scripts, treatments and creative development materials.

04 · CREATE

Explore production

When a project is ready, explore how it could move toward a CrowRules Entertainment production.

THE CREATOR NETWORK

You don't have to build the dream alone.

Dreamscapes is designed to bring different creative skills together around original ideas.

✎

Writers

Help transform concepts into stories, treatments, screenplays, dialogue and narrative worlds.

◉

World Builders

Develop lore, history, cultures, locations, mythology, rules and the deeper structure of a world.

✦

Artists & Designers

Help visualize characters, environments, logos, concepts, props and the visual identity of a project.

🎬

Filmmakers

Explore directing, cinematography, editing, production concepts and visual storytelling.

◎

Storytellers

Help shape characters, emotional arcs, themes, structure and the heart of the story.

∞

Dreamers

The original submitters. The people whose ideas become the starting point for the journey.

CREATOR PARTICIPATION

The dreamer stays part of the dream.

Dreamscapes is designed around transparent collaboration and clear project participation.

Original Creator

The person who submits an idea can remain involved throughout the development process if they choose.

Creative Collaboration

Collaborators contribute their skills while working together to develop the project's creative direction.

Project Records

Dreamscapes can maintain development records, contributor information, credits and project history.

Clear Agreements

Project-specific participation, rights, credits and production arrangements should be documented clearly when appropriate.

Help build Dreamscapes.

Dreamscapes is beginning as a growing creative community. Some early collaborators may participate on a volunteer, unpaid basis while the network and CrowRules Entertainment develop.

Volunteer participation does not guarantee future employment, payment or a paid position. Any future paid work would be handled separately through appropriate employment or contractor arrangements.
