Account Controls

Account-level actions. Project records, agreements, credits and financial records may have separate retention requirements.

Before closing or deleting an account, review your active dreams, projects, agreements, credits, compensation records, messages and files. Closing an account should not be treated as automatically terminating project agreements or deleting records that CrowRules is required or permitted to retain.

Creator Rights & Project Records

Settings do not replace project agreements.

KEEP YOUR CREATIVE RECORDS CONNECTED

Dream submissions, development contributions, credits, agreements, compensation and production records should remain linked to the appropriate project. Changes to your profile do not automatically change ownership, credit, compensation, licensing or other project rights. Those matters should be governed by the applicable written agreement and project record.
