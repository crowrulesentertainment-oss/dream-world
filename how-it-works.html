THE PROCESS

One Dream.
Many Possibilities.

Not every dream becomes a production, and not every project follows exactly the same path. The journey can change as the idea develops.

01

Dream

You bring us the dream. It might be a story, character, scene, world, nightmare, concept, conversation, image, or something you cannot quite explain yet.

Your Imagination Your Starting Point
02

Discovery

If a submission moves forward, CrowRules can talk with the creator about what they imagined, what inspired it, what matters most, and what they would like the dream to become.

Conversation Creator Vision
03

Concept

The raw dream begins taking shape. Characters, themes, settings, story possibilities, tone, genre, format and audience can begin to emerge.

Worldbuilding Characters Story
04

Development

CrowRules and the creator work together to develop the project. This can include outlines, scripts, treatments, character development, visual concepts, episode ideas, research and repeated revisions.

Writing Revisions Creative Collaboration
05

Build

The project becomes more than an idea. A screenplay, series bible, audio script, pitch package, character designs, proof of concept, trailer or other development materials may be created.

Proof Creative Package
06

Funding

When a project is ready, CrowRules can explore appropriate ways to finance development or production. Funding may involve internal resources, grants, sponsorships, crowdfunding, partnerships or other lawful financing.

Funding Partnerships Production Planning
07

Production

The dream enters the real world. Depending on the project, this could mean filming, recording, animation, game development, publishing, photography, music production or another creative production process.

Production Crew Creation
08

Release

When the finished project is ready, it can be released through CrowRules TV, CrowRules Publishing, podcasting, digital platforms, theatrical or festival opportunities, or other appropriate distribution channels.

Premiere Distribution
TIME IS PART OF CREATION

Some Dreams
Take Years.

That's okay. CrowRules Dream World is designed around the idea that meaningful creative work should not be forced into an artificial deadline.

5+ Years? That's Possible.

A project might take months to develop. Another might take five years, ten years, or longer. A creator may need time. Funding may take time. A script may need twenty rewrites. Technology may change. Production schedules may shift. A project may pause and return later.

Develop Slowly Ideas can mature through research, writing, collaboration and revision.
Pause When Necessary Projects can enter a paused state without automatically becoming forgotten.
Resume When Ready Development can continue when the creator, team, resources and timing align.
LONG-TERM DEVELOPMENT

Dreams Don't
Expire Because Life Happens.

Creative projects live in the real world. People have jobs, families, other projects, financial limitations, health challenges, creative changes and unexpected responsibilities.

II

Paused

A project can be placed on hold when circumstances make continued development impractical. Its development history can remain preserved while the project waits.

▶

Resumed

When the creator and project team are ready, development can resume. The project can continue from its previous stage rather than starting over.

↻

Reimagined

Sometimes development reveals that the original format was not the best one. A movie may become a series. A story may become an audio drama. A short may become something much larger.

∞

Long-Term Archive

Projects that do not immediately become productions can remain part of the Dream World archive, subject to the creator's rights, privacy settings and applicable agreements.

IMPORTANT

Creative Collaboration
Requires Clarity.

Your Dream. Clear Agreements.

Submitting a dream does not automatically mean that CrowRules owns it. The submission process, privacy choices and applicable terms determine how a submission may be handled.

If CrowRules decides to develop a project with a creator, the parties should establish the applicable rights, responsibilities, credits, compensation, licensing, ownership and other terms in appropriate written agreements.

A project may involve additional writers, producers, artists, performers, composers, designers or other collaborators. Their contributions and rights should also be documented appropriately.

CrowRules Dream World is a creative-development platform, not a guarantee that every submission will be accepted, developed, produced or released.

Nothing on this page replaces project-specific legal agreements or professional legal advice.
