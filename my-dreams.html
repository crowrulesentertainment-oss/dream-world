WHAT HAPPENS TO A DREAM?

01 · SUBMIT

Your dream enters the CrowRules Dream World and receives its own record and identification code.

02 · DISCOVER

If selected for further development, CrowRules can work directly with you to explore what the dream could become.

03 · DEVELOP

Characters, worlds, stories, scripts, visuals and production plans can evolve over time.

04 · BUILD

A project may become a film, series, audio drama, book, game, short or another creative format.

05 · PRODUCE

Funding, casting, production and post-production can happen when the project is ready.

06 · RELEASE

When a finished project is released, your approved creator credits and applicable compensation are tracked according to the project's agreements.
