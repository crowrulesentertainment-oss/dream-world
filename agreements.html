CrowRules Dream World is designed around collaboration. When a project moves forward, the creator should understand what is happening, who is involved, what has been agreed to and what still needs to be decided.

The exact legal rights, ownership, licenses, credits, compensation, revenue participation and other terms belong in the applicable written agreements for the project.

Important

AGREEMENTS ARE PROJECT-SPECIFIC

A general submission does not automatically establish every ownership, credit, compensation or licensing term.

When CrowRules and a creator decide to develop a project, appropriate agreements should be reviewed and executed for that relationship.
